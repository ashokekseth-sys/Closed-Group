Company Overview

What They Do

Deepak Fertilisers (DFPCL) makes industrial chemicals and crop-nutrition fertilisers. The chemicals side is technical ammonium nitrate (TAN), the explosive base used in mining and quarrying, plus nitric acid and isopropyl alcohol (IPA). The fertiliser side is NPK/ANP bulk grades, traded fertilisers, and specialty crop products. IIFL calls it India's top producer of TAN, nitric acid and IPA; no market-share percentage is given by IIFL or Systematix, and a secondary-source claim of ~45% of merchant nitric acid is unverified.

FY26 revenue was ₹11,506 Cr (+12%); TTM sales are ₹12,432 Cr (MarketSmith ₹12,431.67 Cr, my sum of four quarters ₹12,431.8 Cr). Chemicals were 46% of FY26 revenue but 73% of segment EBIT (my arithmetic from IIFL: 53,042 / 115,060 and 11,466 / (11,466 + 4,199)).

Key competitive advantage (broker-reported, not independently verified): a captive ammonia plant at Taloja (via Performance Chemiserve) fed by a 15-year Equinor LNG contract (~0.65 mtpa, deliveries from 2026), which management calls a structural cost cushion, plus an eastern-India TAN import-substitution position (Gopalpur, 376 ktpa, ~98% complete). IIFL estimates ammonia-plant savings of ₹855.9 Cr in FY27E against ₹49.3 Cr in FY26 (my conversion from Rs mn).
Investment Highlights (v2 additions)

Growth catalyst, new plants early next year: Gopalpur TAN 376 ktpa (₹2,680 Cr, ~98% complete; IIFL says it adds 60-70% to TAN sales) and Dahej nitric acid 300 ktpa WNA + 150 ktpa CNA (₹1,980 Cr, ~98% complete), together about ₹4,660 Cr. Commercial commissioning is guided for 4Q FY27 (Jan-Mar 2027), i.e. early next year. Management's ramp is 75% then 85% then above 95%. Caution: this slipped one quarter from end-2Q FY27, and only a partial quarter lands in 4Q FY27, so the full earnings effect falls in FY28. The risk rows in section 16 are unchanged.

Lower input cost, Equinor LNG: supply is insulated from the Gulf by origin (verified); the size of the cost benefit is the user's thesis plus IIFL's savings estimate, with pricing formula and shipping route unconfirmed (details in section 02).

What This Moat Does NOT Protect Against

Competition arrives: IIFL says a domestic competitor's TAN capacity was commissioned and caused “initial market disruption” (unquantified, competitor not named by IIFL). Chambal Fertilisers started TAN commercial production at Gadepan in Aug 2026 (Screener-side peer research; it is the likely candidate, but that is my inference, not a broker statement). TAN volume was 126.2 kt in Q2 FY27, down 8% YoY and 4% QoQ even at 93% utilisation.

Synthesis: the moat is a cost and integration edge plus an eastern-India import-substitution position, not a patent or brand moat. Eastern India plus Singrauli is about 55% of domestic ammonium nitrate demand with historically high import dependence (IIFL). The edge is real but is being tested by new domestic supply and by the lagged pass-through of ammonia prices, so its durability is unproven in a normal-margin year.
