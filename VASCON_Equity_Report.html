Company Overview

What They Do

Vascon Engineers Limited, Pune (incorporated January 1986; promoters the Vasudevan Ramamoorthy family, per CRISIL), runs two businesses: EPC — buildings, hospitals and medical colleges, institutional, industrial and IT-park construction, mostly for government and state-linked clients — and real estate (Mumbai, Pune, Coimbatore). Group CEO Dr Santosh Sundararajan; Real Estate CEO Raveesh Rao; CFO Somnath Biswas (Q1 FY27 call, 14 Aug 2026). The company has an unmodified audit opinion and exits from non-core assets (for example the GMP Technical Solutions sale, a ₹74.79 Cr gain in FY25 per CRISIL).

FY26 (consolidated): revenue ₹948.53 Cr (company; Screener ₹949 Cr), −12%; company EBITDA ₹87.13 Cr (9%) including ₹35.19 Cr of other income, versus Screener operating profit ₹54 Cr (6%) and CRISIL operating margin 5.7% — three EBITDA definitions, all shown. PAT ₹48.90 Cr (company) / ₹49 Cr (Screener) / ₹54 Cr (CRISIL) — the ₹54 Cr gap is unresolved. Q1 FY27 (reported 12 Aug 2026): sales ₹151.94 Cr (−31%), EBITDA margin 3.25%, PAT ₹2.0 Cr (−91%); management called it ‘one of the worst quarters we’ve had over the last 3 to 4 years’. EPC order book ₹2,850 Cr at 30 Jun 2026 (external ₹2,531 Cr, ~77% government; internal ₹319 Cr for the company’s own real-estate projects), about 3x FY26 EPC revenue (company deck).

Segment split — FY26 full-year: NOT FOUND. In Q1 FY27 EPC was ₹148 Cr of revenue (97%) and real estate ₹4 Cr (EBITDA −₹4 Cr, PBT −₹5 Cr); Q1 FY26 was ₹203 Cr and ₹18 Cr. Management said EPC did ‘INR900 crores plus’ in FY26 and that real-estate revenue ‘did not come in last year’. Real estate: bookings ₹113 Cr in FY26 and ₹66 Cr in Q1 FY27 (30,690 sq ft); collections ₹119 Cr in FY26 (₹58 Cr in FY25, ₹127 Cr in FY24, per CRISIL); cumulative bookings ₹396 Cr against ₹980 Cr of inventory (₹810 Cr launched); unsold inventory ₹414 Cr (deck slide 34) — slide 35 shows ₹228 Cr as Vascon’s share, both are in the deck. Pipeline: Prakash CHS (₹350 Cr GDV, launch planned October 2026), Tower of Future, Baner and a 4-acre JV; about 45% of a 150-acre Thane parcel is held, with the government corridor acquisition at ‘no news’ for 3–6 months (call).

Key Competitive Advantage: Pre-qualification and track record for government hospital, medical-college and institutional building work — about 77% of the ₹2,531 Cr external book is government — plus, until this year, a lightly geared balance sheet (CRISIL adjusted debt/net worth 0.26x in FY26). The edge is credentials, not cost: contracts are tendered, the Reliance award was dropped before work began and EPC margins are 6% (Screener) to 9% (company, including other income). The credit rating is back to BBB+/Stable — the level CRISIL assigned in July 2023, i.e. the October 2024 upgrade has been reversed. Detail in §02 and §16.
