Company Overview

What They Do

NCC Limited (formerly Nagarjuna Construction Company), Hyderabad, is an EPC and construction contractor with more than 40 years in business. It builds buildings, transportation (roads, bridges, metro), mining, electrical T&D, water and railways, and irrigation projects, mostly for government and state-linked clients, across 18 states. It also holds equity in three smart-meter SPVs (three projects, ₹5,011 Cr; ~7–8 mn meters, ~45% installed). International work is negligible: FY26 revenue outside India was ₹13.69 Cr of ₹20,823 Cr.

FY26 (company-reported, consolidated) revenue was ₹20,823 Cr (−6%), EBITDA ₹1,836 Cr (8.82%) and PAT ₹724 Cr, EPS ₹10.76. Whalesbook shows attributable profit of ₹675.32 Cr (−17.4% from ₹819.88 Cr): Screener’s ₹724 Cr is before minority interests (same pattern in FY25: ₹868 Cr vs ₹819.88 Cr). Q1 FY27 (reported 6 Aug 2026): turnover ₹5,842 Cr per the call (Screener sales ₹5,812 Cr), EBITDA ₹545 Cr (9.38%), PAT ₹216.40 Cr attributable (Screener ₹229 Cr before minorities). Consolidated order book ₹81,214 Cr at 30 Jun 2026 (standalone ₹71,312 Cr; subsidiaries ₹9,902 Cr; opening ₹83,004 Cr at 31 Mar 2026) — about 3.9x FY26 revenue (my arithmetic).

Key Competitive Advantage: Scale and balance-sheet capacity to pre-qualify for large state and central tenders, plus lender trust: CARE AA-/Stable and India Ratings AA-/Stable, with ₹20,984 Cr of non-fund-based limits. The edge is qualification and financing capacity, not cost — tenders are awarded competitively and margins sit around 9%. Concentration is built into the book: ~79% is state or state-linked clients, and CARE caps any single agency at 25% of the book. Detail in §02.
